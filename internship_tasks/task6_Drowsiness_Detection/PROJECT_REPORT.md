**Submitted by:** Priti Vijay Singh

## 1. Abstract
This report details the implementation of the Vehicle Drowsiness Detection Model. The system identifies multiple occupants in vehicle images or video frames, tracks alertness status, highlights sleeping individuals in red bounding boxes, predicts their age, and triggers instant alert pop-up messages.
